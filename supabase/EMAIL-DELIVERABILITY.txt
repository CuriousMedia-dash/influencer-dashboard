WHY BRAND MAIL LANDS IN SPAM — AND WHAT FIXES IT
================================================

Almost all of this is DNS and sender setup, not app code. Nothing in the
codebase can talk a spam filter round.

CHECK FIRST: what address is it sending from?
---------------------------------------------
In Supabase: Edge Functions → send-acquisition-mail → Settings →
look at RESEND_FROM.

If it looks like onboarding@resend.dev, or any address NOT on
curiousmedia.in, that alone explains the spam folder. Mail claiming to
be from your agency but sent from someone else's domain is exactly what
filters are built to catch.

THE FIX, IN ORDER OF IMPACT
---------------------------
1. Verify curiousmedia.in in Resend
   Resend dashboard → Domains → Add Domain → curiousmedia.in.
   It gives you three DNS records to add wherever curiousmedia.in is
   managed:
     - DKIM   (a TXT record — this is the big one)
     - SPF    (a TXT record)
     - Return-Path / MX for bounce handling
   Wait for all three to show Verified.

2. Send from a real, consistent address on that domain
   Set RESEND_FROM to something like:
       Curious Media <hello@curiousmedia.in>
   Named sender, real mailbox, same every time. Don't rotate it.

3. Add a DMARC record
   A TXT record at _dmarc.curiousmedia.in:
       v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@curiousmedia.in
   Start at p=none. It monitors without blocking anything.

4. Warm it up
   A brand new domain sending a burst of mail looks like spam. For the
   first week or two, keep volumes low and steady.

CONTENT THINGS THAT MATTER
--------------------------
- A mail that is mostly one link is a classic spam signal. Give it a
  real sentence or two of context around the link.
- Avoid link shorteners entirely.
- Include a plain-text version, not only HTML. The app's mail already
  sends plain text.
- Don't put the brand's own domain name in the subject line.

TESTING IT
----------
Send one to a test address at https://www.mail-tester.com — it scores
the mail out of 10 and tells you exactly which check failed. Anything
above 8 lands in the inbox at most places. Do this after step 1 and
again after step 3.
