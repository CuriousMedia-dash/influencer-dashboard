WHY THE BRAND MAIL GOES TO SPAM (and Resend being verified doesn't help)
========================================================================

THE CAUSE
---------
The mail the brand gets — their login and password-reset link — is NOT
sent through Resend. It's sent by Supabase's own built-in email service.

Supabase's default mail service is a shared sender used by every free
project on the platform. It has no connection to curiousmedia.in, so
none of your verified DNS applies to it. It's also capped at a handful
of mails per hour. Filters treat it exactly as you'd expect.

That's why verifying your domain in Resend changed nothing here: your
acquisition mail goes through Resend, but the brand's login mail never
touched it.

THE FIX — point Supabase's auth mail at Resend too
--------------------------------------------------
About five minutes, no code.

1. In Resend: API Keys → create one (or reuse the existing key). Copy it.

2. In Supabase: Project Settings → Authentication → SMTP Settings →
   turn on "Enable Custom SMTP", and enter:

       Host:            smtp.resend.com
       Port:            465
       Username:        resend
       Password:        <your Resend API key>
       Sender email:    hello@curiousmedia.in   (any verified address)
       Sender name:     Curious Media

   Save.

3. Still in Supabase: Authentication → Rate Limits. The default email
   limit is very low because of the shared service. Now that you're on
   your own SMTP you can raise it — 100/hour is a sensible start.

4. Test it: open the brand login page, hit "forgot password" for a test
   address, and check where it lands. It should now arrive from
   curiousmedia.in and sit in the inbox.

WHAT THIS ALSO FIXES
--------------------
Every automatic mail Supabase sends starts coming from your domain, not
just the brand's: password resets for your own team, and the reset links
the new Team screen sends.

ONE MORE THING
--------------
The share link itself (the /brand/... URL you copy) isn't emailed by the
app at all — you paste it into a mail yourself. If THAT mail is the one
landing in spam, it's your own mailbox's reputation, not the portal, and
the fix is different. Tell me if so.
