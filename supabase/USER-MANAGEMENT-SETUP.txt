TEAM ACCESS — what to do before it works
========================================

1. Deploy the new function. In the Supabase dashboard: Edge Functions →
   Deploy a new function → name it exactly:

       manage-users

   Then paste in everything from
   supabase/functions/manage-users/index.ts and deploy.

   (Or from a terminal: supabase functions deploy manage-users)

2. Check the function's secrets. Edge Functions → manage-users →
   Settings. It needs:

       SUPABASE_URL                 (usually there already)
       SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY    (usually there already)
       RESEND_API_KEY               (same one the mail function uses)
       RESEND_FROM                  (same one the mail function uses)

   The last two are only for sending sign-in details and reset links.
   Without them the accounts still work, you just pass the password on
   yourself.

3. Make sure you're in the admins table, or you'll be locked out of the
   screen you just built:

       select * from admins;

   If your email isn't there:

       insert into admins (email) values ('you@curiousmedia.in');

That's it. The Team button (two-person icon) appears in the top bar for
admins only.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
- Lists everyone with portal access, their level, and when they last
  signed in
- Add someone with a temporary password; they can sign in immediately
  and the details are mailed to them
- Switch anyone between Member and Admin at any time
- Mail a password reset link, which points at
  https://creators.curiousmedia.in
- Remove access entirely

You can't change or remove your own access from here — that's the one
mistake that would lock every admin out.

Brand logins are deliberately not listed; they're a separate thing.
